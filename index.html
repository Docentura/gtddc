    The power of your card.
    On iPhone.

    Apple Pay lets you pay with your credit or debit card on your iPhone, giving you your card’s great benefits and more. You can check out faster online, in apps, and in-store — with just your Apple device. No wallet? No worries. And Apple Pay’s more secure with features like Face ID, making it safer than using just your physical card.

    Get more with every tap.

    Pay in store, online,
    and in apps.

    Pay in store, online, and in apps.

    Just look for the contactless symbol or Apple Pay button at checkout. It’s accepted at over 85% of retail stores nationwide and across millions of apps and websites.

    Security you
    can trust.

    Security you can trust.

    Shop securely with advanced protections built into your iPhone’s hardware and software. Every payment requires authentication — Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

    Earn the same rewards as your physical card.

    Earn the same rewards as your physical card.

    With Apple Pay, your physical card is on your iPhone — so you’ll still earn the same rewards and points your card offers.

    Fly past checkout forms. Online and in apps.

    Fly past checkout forms. Online and in apps.

    Skip tedious forms and breeze through checkout — no need to manually enter your card details, address, or personal information. It works in Safari and even other browsers.1

    Your purchases stay private.

    Your purchases stay private.

    Transaction data remains between you, the merchant, and your bank or card issuer. Apple doesn’t retain transaction information that can be tied back to you. And your data isn’t sold by Apple to third parties for marketing or advertising.

    Designed to protect your card details.

    Designed to protect your card details.

    Merchants never see your original card number — because it’s never sent to them. Apple Pay uses unique transaction codes and device-specific numbers to reduce your exposure to fraud.

    Lock it if you
    lose it.

    Lock it if you lose it.

    Your cards stay secure, even if you misplace your device. Simply put your device in Lost Mode in the Find My app. This disables Apple Pay on that device, putting a temporary hold on your card or cards. And if any of your Apple devices are confirmed lost or stolen, you can remotely erase your information from that specific device.

    Learn more about keeping your cards secure.

    Pay now, later, or with rewards.

    Easy to set up. Easy to pay.

    • Step 01

      Open the Wallet App.

      Go to the Wallet app on your iPhone.

      Step 02

      Tap the the plus symbol.

      You may be asked to sign into your Apple Account.

      Step 03

      Choose a card to add.

      Tap Debit or Credit Card and tap Continue.

      Step 04

      Tap your card.

      Hold your iPhone near the chip on your card, tap,4 or enter card details manually.

      Step 05

      Finish your setup.

      Follow the prompts in the Wallet app to verify your card. Once it’s verified, you’re good to go.

    • Step 01

      Look for the contactless payment symbol.

      Use Apple Pay wherever contactless payments are accepted.

      Step 02

      Double-click the side button.

      No need to unlock your iPhone. Just double-click the side button.

      Step 03

      Confirm it's you.

      Every payment gets authenticated with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

      Step 04

      Complete your payment.

      Hold the top of your iPhone near the card reader until you see Done or a checkmark on the screen.

    • Step 01

      Look for the contactless payment symbol.

      Use Apple Pay wherever contactless payments are accepted.

      Step 02

      Double-click the side button.

      Just double-click the side button on your Apple Watch.

      Step 03

      Complete your payment.

      Hold your Apple Watch a few centimeters from the contactless reader, display facing it, until you feel a gentle tap and hear a beep.

    • Step 01

      Select Apple Pay at checkout.

      Tap the Apple Pay button or choose Apple Pay as your payment method.

      Step 02

      Confirm your personal information.

      Double-check your billing, shipping, and contact information or, if necessary, enter it manually.

      Step 03

      Confirm it’s you.

      Every payment gets authenticated with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.

      Step 04

      Your purchase is complete.

      You’ll get a confirmation at the email you used at checkout. You can also track your order in Wallet to see delivery updates, payment details, and merchant information.
